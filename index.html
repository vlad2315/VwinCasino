How the Upgrader works

Pick a gift from your inventory and a pricier target. The wheel has three sectors: green = win (you get the target and the donor gift burns), yellow = refund (nothing changes, you keep your gift), red = lose (your gift burns).

Upgrade paths: only gifts worth up to 100 ★ take part. A 15 ★ gift can become a 25 ★ or 50 ★ gift; a 25 ★ gift a 50 ★ or 100 ★ gift; a 50 ★ gift any 100 ★ gift. How Gift Spinner works

Only the winning cells are highlighted.

Gift values are fixed star prices (Teddy Bear 15 ★, Dog in Cap 627 ★, …) and do not depend on the spin price; "Sell" pays exactly that value. Bets. The minimum base bet is 1% of your balance, but never less than 10 stars. A random spin costs the base bet; of random spins land on nothing and the stake is lost.
