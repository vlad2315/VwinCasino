How Neon Cyber Crash works

Place a bet and launch. The rocket climbs and the multiplier grows. Press Cash Out before it crashes and you win your bet times the multiplier on screen; if it crashes first, the bet is lost. You can also set an auto cash-out, which pays out at exactly the multiplier you typed.

The crash point is drawn from the browser's secure random generator when you launch: m = floor(93 / U) / 100, at least ×1.00, with U uniform in (0, 1]. That makes the chance of reaching any multiplier x exactly 93% / x, so every strategy returns 93.00% in the long run. A round is capped at ×10,000 and pays out automatically there.

Как работает Upgrader

Выберите подарок из инвентаря и более дорогой подарок-цель. Колесо делится на три сектора: зелёный — успех (цель падает в инвентарь, ваш подарок сгорает), жёлтый — возврат (ничего не меняется, подарок остаётся у вас), красный — проигрыш (подарок сгорает).

Шансы зависят от отношения цен r = ваш подарок / цель: успех = 10% + 80%·r, возврат = 20%·(1 − r), остальное — проигрыш. Например, 25 ★ → 50 ★: 50% / 10% / 40%; 15 ★ → 25 ★: 58% / 8% / 34%. Чем дороже цель, тем ниже шанс. Это демо на виртуальных звёздах.

How Tunnel Plunge works

Press DROP. The dial spins: it lands on a pickaxe or, in random mode, on nothing at all. A pickaxe falls into an endless mine, bounces off the blocks and wears them down. Each touch costs the pickaxe some HP. The round only ends when the HP runs out; there is no floor. Ores, chests and TNT pay a share of your base bet. Round wins are capped at the base bet.

Bets. The minimum base bet is 1% of your balance, but never less than 10 stars. A random spin costs the base bet; of random spins land on nothing and the stake is lost. A guaranteed pickaxe costs the base bet times its price factor (see the table: each price is that pickaxe's measured expected win divided by 0.93, so every mode returns 93%). Change the base bet and every price follows it. Ores always pay a share of the base bet.

Crafting tables upgrade the pickaxe to the next tier (if it is not the best one) and restore its HP to 100%. TNT blasts a wide area and throws the pickaxe. Stone always breaks on the first touch. Every touch costs exactly 1 pickaxe HP. Slime blocks take 7 touches and bounce the pickaxe 2.2 times harder. Size boosters (stone with a green arrow) break in one touch and make the pickaxe 1.5 times bigger for 4 seconds. The drop column is always random.
